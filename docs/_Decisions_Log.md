- **2026-11-01 · Gym smart order: the tired score** (Ahmed, over a simple rotation and a fixed fair cycle). A planned workout can reorder its exercises from his last 3 workouts of it, as he really did them, so the exercises that keep landing late get the fresh start. Claude's calls, in the short design waiting for his yes: the score is how many workouts in a row (up to 3) an exercise has gone without a slot in the first third, longest wait first, ties to the one done later or skipped (a plain "late goes first" rule leaves the middle exercises in the middle forever); two exercises for the same muscle never back to back; the "big lift before small lift" rule is dropped (the 2021 order review found muscle growth similar either way, and the rule would push small lifts back to the end); it is demo-first, with no database change. Source: this chat; Nunes et al. 2021, Eur J Sport Sci 21(2):149–157.
- **2026-11-01 · Gym smart order: mix the muscles, helpers last** (Ahmed, before agreeing to the design). Two exercises of the same family (push: chest, shoulders, triceps; pull: back, biceps; legs; core) never next to each other unless no order can avoid it, so he rests less; triceps after every chest and shoulder exercise and biceps after every back exercise, because a tired helper muscle weakens the big lifts (a 2016 study backs it: triceps pushdowns first cut the bench press reps, JSSM 15:111). This replaces Claude's call earlier the same day to drop the "big lift before small lift" rule. Claude's additions, in the design waiting for his yes: forearms with the biceps, core after legs and back, families from the main muscle (his machines list no helper muscles), an exact search so an early choice never corners the order, and a "First up: one light set first" line. Source: this chat; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-gym-smart-order-design.md`.
